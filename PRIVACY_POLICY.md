# Privacy Policy for Asraisaya

**Last Updated: September 11, 2024**

## Introduction

Welcome to Asraisaya ("we", "us", "our", or "Company"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our online information practices and the choices you can make about the way your information is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- Username
- Email address
- Profile information
- Quiz scores and performance data
- Device information (for analytics)

### Non-Personal Information
- Device type and operating system
- Gameplay statistics
- Device identifiers
- Crash reports
- Analytics data

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and improve the game services
- To maintain leaderboards and rankings
- To send you notifications and rewards
- To analyze game usage and performance
- To fix bugs and technical issues
- To prevent fraud and ensure security

## 3. Data Storage and Security

- All data is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS
- Data is stored securely on Firebase servers
- We implement multi-layer security measures
- Access is restricted to authorized personnel
- Regular security audits are conducted

## 4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

- **Firebase** (Google): For authentication and database
- **Google AdMob**: For displaying advertisements
- **Google Analytics**: For usage analytics
- **Facebook**: For social sharing features

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request data deletion
- Opt-out of marketing communications
- Request data export

## 6. Children's Privacy

Asraisaya is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete such information and terminate the child's account.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
